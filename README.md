# CRM Sales Analysis – Salesforce + Google Sheets

## Project Overview
This project focused on analyzing sales performance, customer activity, and product trends using Salesforce CRM, Google Sheets, and Excel. The goal was to create a simple CRM sales management and reporting system while building dashboards and visualizations to support business decision-making.

## Objectives
- Track sales opportunities and customer activity
- Analyze product performance and sales trends
- Build dashboards and visual reports
- Improve data organization and reporting processes
- Practice CRM management and data analysis skills

## Tools Used
- Salesforce CRM
- Google Sheets
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Charts & Dashboards
- Data Visualization
- Data Cleaning

## Dataset
The dataset included:
- Product names
- Sales revenue
- Customer activity
- Sales pipeline information
- Opportunity stages
- Lead management data

## Key Findings
- GTX Basic generated the highest sales volume
- MG Special and GTXPro also showed strong product performance
- Sales dashboards helped identify top-performing products and trends
- CRM opportunity tracking improved visibility into the sales pipeline

## Project Features
- Created sales dashboards and reports
- Built pivot tables and visual charts
- Organized CRM opportunity and lead tracking
- Cleaned and structured sales data
- Used dashboards to visualize business insights

## Visualizations
The project includes:
- Sales performance dashboards
- Product sales charts
- CRM opportunity reports
- Pivot table analysis
- Salesforce reports and dashboards

## Conclusion
This project strengthened my skills in CRM systems, sales analysis, dashboard creation, and business reporting using Salesforce, Google Sheets, and Excel. It also improved my ability to organize and analyze business data to support data-driven decisions.
